About Krish Metal Corporation

Bending tools built by people who understand bending

Established in 2016 in Bhosari, Pune, Krish Metal Corporation manufactures and services Press Brake Tools, Forming Tool Dies, Shearing Machine Knives and Machine Guide-ways, and offers a Shearing Blade Resharpening Service.

We supply tooling for every press brake process – air bending, coining / stamping and bottom bending – with clamping that suits most press brakes in use today. Alongside our standard range we design custom geometries for parts that standard tools cannot make.

  • Standard KMC / EURO profiles in 835 / 415 mm solid and 805 mm sectioned lengths
  • Induction hardened working edges, 55–60 HRC
  • Tool selection help, tonnage checks and DXF files at no extra cost

Mission & Vision

Making every bend right the first time

From a single punch to a full machine set, we aim to give fabricators tooling that is accurate, durable and delivered when it is needed.

To manufacture press brake tooling that holds its accuracy shift after shift, to stock the profiles our customers use most, and to support every order with practical engineering advice – so Indian fabricators can bend faster, scrap less and stay competitive.

To be the first name sheet-metal workshops across India think of for press brake tools – a Pune-based manufacturer known for precision, fast delivery and custom solutions that match the quality of imported tooling.
