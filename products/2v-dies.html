KMC / EURO Style

2-V (Self-Centering) Dies

ECONOMY · C4555–60 HRC

Our 2-V system mounts a reversible double-V die in a self-centering holder. Flip the die to change between two V openings; the holder keeps the V centred under the punch every time.

Types

  • PDD-001H holders, H 55 – 75 mm
  • PDD-002 / 003 / 004 dies H 26 mm (90°, 88°, 60°)
  • PDD-005 30° die H 46 mm
  • PDD-006H adapter + PDS-001 stopper
  • PDD-007 / 008 / 009 dies H 46 mm

Features

  • Two V openings in one die
  • Automatic centring – no re-alignment
  • Ratings up to 100 t/m
