Our Story

From a Pune workshop to tooling for machines across India

Krish Metal Corporation was established in 2016 in the Landge Industrial Estate, Bhosari – at the heart of Pune’s automotive and engineering belt. We manufacture and service a focused range of products: Press Brake Tools, Forming Tool Dies, Shearing Machine Knives and Machine Guide-ways, and we run a Shearing Blade Resharpening Service.

Our customers range from small job shops with a single press brake to OEMs running several lines. What they share is a need for tools that bend accurately, last long and arrive quickly – and that is exactly what we build our business around.

Press Brake Bending

What our tools do

Press brake bending is the most widely used way to form sheet metal cold. A hydraulic or electric press drives the beam and upper punch into a lower die, applying exactly the force needed to deform the sheet to the required angle and shape.

Air Bending

The punch pushes the sheet into the V without bottoming. Flexible and low-tonnage – one die opening covers many angles.

Bottom Bending

The sheet is pressed against the die flanks for a more repeatable angle, using matched punch and die angles.

Coining / Stamping

High force imprints the punch tip into the material for the tightest angles and radii.

Because the result depends on the geometry of the bend and of the tools, we supply the right profile for each process – and our standard clamping fits most press brakes used worldwide. Where a standard geometry will not do, we manufacture custom tooling.

Quality

Induction hardening & material grades

Every tool passes through induction hardening so the areas that touch the part during bending get maximum protection. Declared hardness is 55–60 HRC with a hardened depth of 3–4 mm.
