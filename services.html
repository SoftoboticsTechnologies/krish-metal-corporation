Our Services

Engineering support and after-sales services that keep your press brakes and shears productive.

What We Do

More than tooling

From the first drawing to blade resharpening years later, we support the full life of your bending and cutting tools.

01

Custom Press Brake Tooling to Drawing

Special punches and dies designed from your part drawing – joggles, offsets, special radii, louvers and profiles for non-standard clamping. We propose the geometry, you approve, we manufacture.

Learn more Ask on WhatsApp
02

Shearing Blade Resharpening

Worn shear blades reground to a clean, square cutting edge with controlled stock removal, restoring cut quality and reducing burr at a fraction of the cost of new blades.

Learn more Ask on WhatsApp
03

Shearing Machine Knives & Machine Guide-ways

New shearing knives for guillotine and swing-beam shears, plus machine guide-ways – manufactured to your machine’s drawing or sample.

Learn more Ask on WhatsApp
04

Bending Sequence Visualisation

Unsure if a part can be bent without collisions? On request we simulate the bending sequence with your machine, tooling and part to check for clashes before you buy.

Ask on WhatsApp
05

Detailed Technological Configuration

A full bending plan for your specific machine – every step including backgauge positioning. This engineering service is provided for an additional charge.

Ask on WhatsApp
06

Tool List from Part Drawing

Send your part drawing and we will prepare the complete list of punches, dies and holders needed to make it.

Ask on WhatsApp
07

DXF Tool Geometry for CNC Libraries

For every tool sold we supply its actual 2D geometry in DXF format for import into your CNC controller’s tool library – free of charge.

Ask on WhatsApp
How We Work

Simple, fast, transparent

  1. Share – machine model, part drawing or sample, material and quantity.
  2. Review – our engineers check tonnage, V-opening and clearances.
  3. Quote – a clear quotation with delivery time (prices on request).
  4. Deliver – stock items in 24/48/72h, special tools as committed, with DXF files.
